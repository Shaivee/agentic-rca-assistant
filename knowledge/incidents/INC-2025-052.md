# INC-2025-052: Yellowing from caustic overdose

- **Date:** 2025-12-02
- **Area:** Bleach plant, P stage
- **Symptom:** Brightness fell 1 %ISO and pulp looked yellow.
- **Root cause:** NaOH flow controller setpoint entered as 19 kg/t instead of 15 kg/t, causing
  alkali darkening.
- **Evidence:** caustic_dose at 19 kg/t for six hours; peroxide dose and temperature normal.
- **Corrective action:** Setpoint corrected; operator checklist updated.
