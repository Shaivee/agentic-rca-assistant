# INC-2025-031: Brightness loss from low tower temperature

- **Date:** 2025-12-14
- **Area:** Bleach plant, P stage
- **Symptom:** Brightness drifted down 2 %ISO overnight; peroxide dose normal.
- **Root cause:** Instrument air line to steam valve TV-210 positioner cracked; valve could not
  open fully and tower temperature fell to 68 °C.
- **Evidence:** bleach_temp fell over about 40 minutes; valve position lagged controller output;
  no change in chemical dosing.
- **Corrective action:** Air line replaced, positioner recalibrated.
- **Lesson:** Same symptom as a dosing problem (low brightness) but the dose was normal. Always
  check temperature before blaming chemicals.
