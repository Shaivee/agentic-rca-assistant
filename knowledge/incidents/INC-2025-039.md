# INC-2025-039: Low tensile strength from worn refiner plates

- **Date:** 2025-10-29
- **Area:** Stock preparation, refining
- **Symptom:** Tensile index fell from 65 to 57 Nm/g over a week.
- **Root cause:** Refiner plates worn beyond service life; refining energy could not be applied.
- **Evidence:** Gradual tensile decline tracking refiner plate hours; specific energy below target.
- **Corrective action:** Refiner plates replaced.
