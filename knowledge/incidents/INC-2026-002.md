# INC-2026-002: False high-pH alarms from fouled probe

- **Date:** 2026-01-20
- **Area:** Bleach plant, P stage
- **Symptom:** Repeated bleach_ph HIGH alarms over six hours; operators considered reducing caustic.
- **Root cause:** Reference junction of pH probe AT-305 fouled, causing the reading to drift
  upward. Real pH (grab sample) was 10.8, on target.
- **Evidence:** Slow one-directional pH drift; caustic and peroxide doses normal; brightness and
  all quality results normal; lab grab sample disagreed with online reading by 0.6 pH.
- **Quality impact:** None. Sensor fault only.
- **Corrective action:** Probe cleaned and recalibrated. Caustic dose was correctly NOT changed.
- **Lesson:** Verify the sensor before reacting. Changing caustic would have created a real problem.
