# INC-2026-011: Brightness dip during first use of a new hardwood pulp

- **Date:** 2026-02-17
- **Area:** Bleach plant / raw materials
- **Symptom:** Brightness 1.5–2 %ISO lower for a full day; no process alarms.
- **Root cause:** First full-scale lot from a new hardwood pulp supplier had lower bleachability.
  The supplier had not completed the laboratory bleachability trial required by SOP-QA-02.
- **Evidence:** All bleach plant variables on target; brightness drop started and ended exactly
  with the lot change recorded in the events log.
- **Corrective action:** Switched back to the regular supplier; brightness recovered.
- **Lesson:** When nothing in the process changed, look at what went into the process.
