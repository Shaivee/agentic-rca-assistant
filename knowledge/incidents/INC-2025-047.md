# INC-2025-047: Rising holes and spots from overdue press felt

- **Date:** 2025-10-08
- **Area:** Paper machine, press section
- **Symptom:** WIS defect rate rose steadily from about 2 to 5 defects/km over four days.
- **Root cause:** Press felt PF-1 left in service 25 days (planned life 18) because a shutdown
  was postponed. Felt filling reduced dewatering and caused holes and spots.
- **Evidence:** Gradual ramp in defect rate tracking felt age; defects mostly in a CD band;
  defect rate fell back to normal immediately after the felt change.
- **Corrective action:** Felt changed.
- **Preventive action:** Felt changes may no longer be postponed past planned life without
  production manager approval.
