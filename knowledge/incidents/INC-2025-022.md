# INC-2025-022: Effluent TSS exceedance from disc filter blinding

- **Date:** 2025-09-12
- **Area:** Stock preparation / effluent
- **Symptom:** Effluent TSS rose to 52 mg/L (permit limit 40 mg/L).
- **Root cause:** Screens on disc filter DF-1 blinded with fines after a change in filler dosing.
- **Evidence:** filter_dp rose steadily from 30 to 50 kPa over eight hours; effluent_tss
  followed within the hour.
- **Corrective action:** High-pressure wash of DF-1 screens; TSS normal within two hours.
