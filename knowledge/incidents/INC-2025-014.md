# INC-2025-014: Low brightness after peroxide pump check-valve failure

- **Date:** 2025-11-03
- **Area:** Bleach plant, P stage
- **Symptom:** Brightness fell from 86 to 82.5 %ISO over two batches; two batches failed spec.
- **Root cause:** Discharge check valve on peroxide dosing pump P-301 stuck partly open, so the
  pump recirculated and actual H2O2 dose fell to about 17 kg/t. The flow reading was unsteady.
- **Evidence:** peroxide_dose dropped about 30 minutes before brightness fell; operator noted
  pulsating discharge pressure; check valve found damaged on inspection.
- **Corrective action:** Check valve replaced; dose and brightness recovered within 45 minutes.
- **Preventive action:** Added P-301 to the monthly vibration and inspection route.
- **Lesson:** Unsteady peroxide flow plus a lagged brightness drop points to the dosing pump.
