# INC-2026-007: Repeated web breaks from draw imbalance

- **Date:** 2026-01-05
- **Area:** Paper machine, dryer section
- **Symptom:** Six web breaks in one shift.
- **Root cause:** Speed draw between press and dryer section set too high after a grade change.
- **Evidence:** Breaks started immediately after the grade change; draw setpoint 1.8% higher than
  standard.
- **Corrective action:** Draw reset to standard; breaks stopped.
