# SOP-ENV-01: Effluent Quality and Disc Filter DF-1

## Purpose
Keep effluent total suspended solids (TSS) below the permit limit of 40 mg/L.

## Disc filter DF-1
DF-1 recovers fibre and fines from white water. Its differential pressure (tag filter_dp) is
normally 25–35 kPa; alarm at 42 kPa.

## Failure mode: screen blinding
Fines and fillers gradually block (blind) the filter screens. Differential pressure climbs over
several hours, filtration efficiency falls and solids pass through to the effluent, raising TSS.

## Actions
1. If filter_dp rises steadily, start a high-pressure wash of the DF-1 screens.
2. Check effluent_tss trend; TSS normally follows differential pressure within the same hour.
3. Report any batch with effluent TSS above 40 mg/L to the environmental coordinator.
