# SOP-INS-01: pH Measurement and Probe Maintenance

## Purpose
Ensure bleach tower pH readings (probe AT-305, tag bleach_ph) are trustworthy before acting on them.

## Key rule
**Never change chemical dosing based on a pH alarm alone.** pH probes in bleach liquor foul and
drift. Always confirm with a grab sample measured in the lab before adjusting caustic.

## Signs of probe drift (sensor fault, not process fault)
- pH reading changes slowly and steadily in one direction over several hours.
- Caustic dose, peroxide dose and temperature are all normal.
- Brightness and other quality results stay normal despite the "abnormal" pH.
- A grab sample disagrees with the online reading by more than 0.2 pH.

## Maintenance
- Clean and calibrate AT-305 every 14 days, or immediately if drift is suspected.
- Typical cause of drift: fouled reference junction.

## Impact assessment
If a grab sample confirms the real pH is normal, the event is an instrumentation issue only and
has no product-quality impact. Record it as a sensor fault.
