# SOP-PM-03: Press Felt Management

## Purpose
Planned replacement of press felts and recognising felt wear before it causes quality losses.

## Planned life
Press felt PF-2 has a planned service life of **18 days**. Felts must be changed at or before
planned life, at the next scheduled shutdown.

## Symptoms of a worn or filled felt
- Gradual rise in Web Inspection System (WIS) defect rate over several days, mainly small holes
  and spots, often concentrated in a cross-direction (CD) band.
- Rising press section vacuum, lower dewatering, sometimes more web breaks.
- Defect rate drops sharply immediately after the felt change.

## Distinguish from other causes
A sudden step change in defects points to something else (dirt in the stock, a damaged
doctor blade). Felt wear is a slow ramp that tracks felt age.

## Actions
1. Check felt age against planned life.
2. If overdue, schedule a felt change at the earliest opportunity.
